# Motion Creative Benchmarks 2026

*Source: Motion Creative Benchmarks 2026 (final report + LLM edition)*
*Data window: September 1, 2025 – January 1, 2026 (BFCM window)*
*Dataset: 578,750 ads, 6,015 advertiser accounts, $1.29B in realized spend on Facebook/Instagram*
*Filed: 2026-06-29*

---

## Core Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Winner | Ad with spend ≥10× account median AND ≥$500 |
| Mid-range | ≥28 days spend, does not meet winner threshold |
| Loser | Turned off before 28 days |
| Hit rate | Winner creatives ÷ total creatives × 100 (unweighted) |
| Spend use ratio | Format's % of spend ÷ format's % of creative usage. >1.0 punches above weight |

---

## Key Findings

### Winners are rare — that's normal
- ~4-8% of all ads are winners by spend tier
- ~50-53% are losers
- ~38-46% are mid-range
- The 10× threshold ≈ 92.3rd percentile — expect 1 winner per 10-13 ads on average

### Volume is the only structural lever
More ads launched per week = more winners. Not because quality improves — because more tests means more chances. This holds even controlling for budget.

### Hit rate is misleading without volume context
- 50 ads at 4% hit rate = 2 winners
- 5 ads at 20% hit rate = 1 winner
- High hit rate can mean limited testing, not better creative judgment
- Lower hit rate often appears in accounts that test more and ultimately get more winners

## Winning Formats

### Visual formats — hit rate and spend use ratio
| Format | Hit rate (%) | Spend use ratio | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Offer-First Banner | 8.6 | 1.3 | Scale format — high volume and punches above weight |
| Demo | 8.1 | 1.0 | Earns its place consistently |
| Testimonial | 6.5 | 1.0 | Solid, reliable |
| Unboxing | 9.8 | 1.3 | High hit rate, lower volume |
| Celebrity | 5.9 | 2.1 | Rare but punches way above weight |
| Before & After | In winner band | — | High relevance for service/transformation businesses |
| Founder | High hit rate | Above 1.0 | Works well for personal brands |
| POV | In winner band | — | Authenticity-driven |
| Behind the scenes | In winner band | — | Trust builder |

### Hooks and headlines — what wins
**By hit rate (6-11% band):** Newness, Sale announcement, Price anchor, Urgency, Announcement, Offer only, FOMO, New product announcement, Confession, Exclusivity, Curiosity, Giveaway, Bold claim, Shocking statement

**By spend use ratio (>1.0):** Giveaway, Price anchor, Announcement, Event announcement, Offer only, Confession, Urgency, Curiosity, FOMO, Wordplay

**Pattern:** Hooks signaling immediacy and concrete reason to act outperform narrative/emotional hooks on spend ratio.

### Asset types — what outperforms expectations
**High hit rate:** Text only, Product image with text, Lifestyle-product image, UGC
**High spend use ratio:** Text only, Product image with text, Illustration, UGC

**Key insight:** Text-forward assets outperform what most clients expect. Speed and clarity beat production value for testing. High production = credibility/scaling format, not testing format.

## Application to Damien's Businesses

### Dametime Marketing (client-facing)
- All clients at launch will be micro tier — set 4% hit rate expectations upfront
- Sell testing cadence as the product: minimum 3 ads/week, kill losers fast, double down on winners
- Report in winner/mid-range/loser buckets — makes value legible
- Lead creative strategy with offer-first banners and demo formats
- Text-forward assets > expensive production for micro budgets
- Pitch framing: "I run a volume-and-testing system because that's what the data says produces winners at your budget level"

### Dametime Tattoos / Rook (organic + small paid)
- Best organic formats: Before & After (every session), Demo/process (time-lapses), Testimonial (client on camera)
- Best hooks: Newness ("new flash available"), Urgency ("2 spots open"), Curiosity (partial reveal), Price anchor
- Asset type: phone video with text overlay > fancy graphics. Post the work, let it sell.
- If running paid: micro tier, 3+ creatives/week minimum, offer-first

### Above the Norm (local service, Winnipeg geo-targeted)
- Primary asset: before/after photos — every job, every time
- Best formats: Before & After, Demo (crew working), Testimonial (homeowner on camera)
- Best hooks: Urgency ("book before season ends"), Direct address ("Winnipeg homeowners"), Offer only ("free estimate")
- Asset type: clean photos with text overlays. Clarity and CTA over production value.
- Every ad should drive a direct action: estimate request, DM, call

## Cautions (per report methodology)
- All findings are associations, not causal proof
- Time-bound to BFCM/holiday window — some format advantages may not hold year-round
- Spend is the success metric, not ROAS, CPA, or conversion — interpret accordingly
- Vertical benchmarks apply where sample ≥50 accounts (smaller verticals remapped to "Other")
