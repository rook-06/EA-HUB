## Core Content Philosophy (Hormozi)

**"Give away the secrets, sell the implementation."** Content's job is to demonstrate expertise so thoroughly that when someone doesn't want to do it themselves, Damien is the only logical call.

**Content is the lubricant, not the funnel.** Content builds trust at scale. Direct outreach (warm + cold) is what closes jobs in the short term. Both run simultaneously.

**"Document, don't create" for a solo operator.** Film what you're already doing. Before/after on every job. Process shots during tattoo sessions. Tip videos during downtime. No content studio needed.

## Hook / Retain / Reward Framework

Every piece of content follows this structure:

- **Hook:** Pain-first, under 3 seconds. Name the specific problem the viewer is already feeling. Not a teaser — make them feel seen.
- **Retain:** Keep them through storytelling, a list, or a promised payoff still coming.
- **Reward:** The actual actionable insight or reveal. This is what makes people follow and return. Hollow or generic content fails here.

## Content Accordion (Scaling Without More Hours)

One long-form piece → 30+ distribution assets:
- Shoot one before/after → Reel, TikTok, Facebook post, still photo caption, story
- Record one tip video → cut into 3-5 short-form clips for all platforms
- Minimum viable: one shoot per week, 30-60 minutes, becomes a week of posts

## Brand Tones

**Above the Norm**
- Confident, blue-collar, results-focused
- "We show up and get it done" energy
- Highlights: before/after transformations, client results, seasonal tips for local homeowners
- Hormozi application: tip content ("3 things killing your Chicago lawn in June") positions Damien as the expert homeowners call when they don't want to DIY

**Dametime Tattoos**
- Artistic, personal, community-oriented
- Lets the work speak — captions support the image, don't overshadow it
- Highlights: custom pieces, process/time-lapse shots, healed work, client reactions
- Every caption has one CTA: direct to DM
- Hormozi application: education content (what to expect, how to communicate your idea, healing process) eliminates objections before the client reaches out

**Dametime Marketing**
- Professional but not corporate — entrepreneurial, direct
- Positioning: Damien knows what works because he does it for his own businesses
- Highlights: strategy insights, results from ATN and Dametime Tattoos as real case studies, what other local businesses are getting wrong
- Hormozi application: "give away the secrets" content converts skeptical business owners — they see the expertise, decide they don't want to figure it out themselves, and call
