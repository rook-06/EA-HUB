**Current projects:**
- `projects/marketing-portfolio/` — Building a standout portfolio to attract and close clients

**Target clients:** Likely small business owners who are skeptical of marketing agencies, are underserved, or are doing everything DIY and need help scaling their presence.

**Goal:** Build credibility fast. Each contract won = revenue + portfolio piece + proof of concept.
