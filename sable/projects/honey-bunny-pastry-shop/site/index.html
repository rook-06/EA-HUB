Our Story

From one recipe worth sharing

Honey Bunny started with a single honey-glazed bun recipe, tested batch after batch until it was worth handing to someone else. What began as weekend baking for friends turned into a full case, one tray at a time.

Every pastry is made in small runs and finished the same day: nothing sits in the case overnight.
