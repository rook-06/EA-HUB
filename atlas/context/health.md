## Current Routines

**Standard method for unilateral machines:** 8 reps each side (at max for that rep range) followed immediately by 8 reps bilateral at the same weight. Functions as a mechanical drop set — extends past single-limb failure using bilateral load sharing. Applied to: machine preacher curls, seated/lying leg curls. Apply to any machine that allows unilateral work.

**Weekly structure:**
- Mon — Upper A
- Tue — Lower A
- Wed — Rest + mobility (20 min)
- Thu — Upper B → sprint intervals after (15-20 min)
- Fri — Lower B
- Sat — Rest or second conditioning session (optional)
- Sun — Rest

**DAY 1 — UPPER A (Strength focus)**
- Smith machine or DB bench press — 4x6-8
- DB or cable rows — 4x6-8
- DB shoulder press — 3x8-10
- Lat pulldowns — 3x8-10
- Cable lateral raises — 3x12-15

**DAY 2 — LOWER A (Posterior chain)**
- Hip thrusts — 4x8-10
- DB Romanian deadlifts — 3x10-12
- Leg curls — 3x12-15
- Walking lunges — 3x10 each leg
- Calf raises — 3x20

**DAY 3 — UPPER B (Volume/hypertrophy focus)**
- Incline DB press — 3x10-12
- Lat pulldowns or cable rows — 3x12-15
- Cable chest press (lower focus) — 3x12-15
- Preacher curls — 3x10-12
- Tricep pushdowns — 3x10-12
- Overhead tricep extension — 3x10-12
- Incline hammer curls — 3x12-15

**DAY 4 — LOWER B (Quad dominant + conditioning)**
- Decline leg press — 4x10-12
- Leg extensions — 3x12-15
- Hip thrusts — 3x15 (lighter, higher rep)
- Pistol squats — 3x6-8 each leg (actual work, not warmup)
- Sprint intervals to finish — 8x30m, 90s rest

**Conditioning (1-2x/week, outdoor — separate from gym):**
- 8-10 x 30-40m sprints at full effort, walk back as rest
- Never after a lower day — schedule after Upper A, Upper B, or on a rest day
- Thursday: built in after Upper B
- Saturday: optional second session
- Note: outdoor sprints, not treadmill — this is where the athletic development happens

**Mobility:**
- Wednesday rest day — 15-20 min
- Not immediately before a heavy session (static stretching pre-workout reduces power output)

Planet Fitness constraints (machine/dumbbell/cable heavy — limited barbell access). Hip hinge gap being addressed with hip thrusts on both lower days.
