## Best for

- Personal budget review and adjustments
- Savings goals and timelines
- Spending pattern analysis
- Financial independence planning — what does Damien's dream life actually cost?

**Philosophy:** The goal isn't just money — it's the life the money makes possible. Every financial decision connects to what Damien is actually building toward.
