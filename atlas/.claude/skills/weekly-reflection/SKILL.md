# Skill: Weekly Reflection

A structured weekly personal review — what happened, how things are going, what to adjust.

## When to use

When Damien wants to close out a week and check in on his personal life. This is not a business review — that's Sable's domain. This is about the person.

## How to run

### Step 1 — Load context

Read: `context/me.md`, `context/health.md`, `context/relationships.md`, `context/growth.md`, `context/current-priorities.md`

### Step 2 — Check the bridge

Read `C:\Users\damnm\ai-assistants\ai-bridges\shared-context.md` and `C:\Users\damnm\ai-assistants\ai-bridges\sable-to-atlas.md` for anything from Sable that's relevant to how this week went.

### Step 3 — Run the reflection

Ask Damien these five questions one at a time — not all at once. Listen to the full answer before moving to the next.

1. On a scale of 1–10, how did this week actually feel? Not how productive it was — how it felt.
2. What went well in your personal life this week? (Not work stuff — Sable handles that.)
3. What didn't? Where did something slip or feel off?
4. Any people you wanted to connect with but didn't? Anyone you need to reach out to?
5. Is there anything you're avoiding that this week made harder to ignore?

### Step 4 — Synthesize

Based on his answers, produce:
- One or two observations worth naming
- One specific adjustment for next week — concrete, not aspirational
- Any context files that need updating
- Any message worth sending to Sable via the bridge

### Step 5 — Close out

Suggest the session summary template. Prompt Damien to append any decisions to `decisions/log.md`.
